# Problem Statement

## Current Challenges
1. Dispersed learning materials across fragmented platforms.
2. Lack of personalized progress tracking and study streaks.
3. Disconnect between reading study materials and taking contextual notes.
4. Absence of instant AI-powered doubt resolution during study sessions.

## Proposed Solution
E-Study Corner unifies notes, study materials, courses, quizzes, and AI coaching into a single modern web application.
